About

Your IP, explained.

An IP address is the number the internet uses to route data back to you. Your public IP is visible to every site you visit, and it reveals your approximate location and internet provider.

A WebRTC leak happens when your browser discloses IP addresses — including ones your VPN is trying to hide — through real-time communication features. If the leak test above shows addresses your VPN shouldn't reveal, your setup needs attention.
